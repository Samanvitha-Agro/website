Agro Tech: smarter farming, lower costs

We are building a digital ecosystem that sits alongside the farmer, from deciding what to sow to selling the harvest. The aim is simple: better yield at lower cost.

Farm advisory

Crop, seed and input guidance based on soil, season and local weather, in regional languages.

Cost and yield insights

Simple tracking that shows where money goes and which practices pay back.

Smart water and inputs

Sensor-ready tools to cut water, fertiliser and pesticide use without cutting yield.

Market linkage

Direct links from farm to buyer with traceable lots and transparent pricing.
